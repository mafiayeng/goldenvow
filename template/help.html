Help Center

Everything you need to know about using GoldenVow.

Creating an Event

Click “Create Event” on your dashboard. Fill in the title, description, target amount, and dates. Choose a payment method (Bank, M‑Pesa, Paybill, or Till) – only the relevant fields will appear. Once saved, your event gets a unique shareable link.

Managing Contributors

From your event list, click “Manage” to view all contributors. You can add a contributor manually, or they can register via the event page. When a contributor submits payment proof, you’ll see it under their record – review and click “Approve” to confirm the contribution.

Fees & Referrals

Platform fee: We charge a 2% fee on every approved contribution (this supports platform maintenance and development).
Referral programme: When you refer another admin using your unique referral code, your fee percentage decreases:
1 referral → 1.80%  |  2 referrals → 1.72%  |  4 referrals → 1.61%  |  9+ referrals → 1.54%.

Event Locking & Grace Period

If an event receives contributions with fees totalling at least KES 50, and the fee is not paid within 3 days, the event page will be locked (contributors cannot add new pledges). You can unlock it by paying the fee (contact super admin).

AI Assistant

Click “AI Help” in the navbar to ask questions about your events, total raised, pending approvals, or how to do things. If the AI can’t answer, your question is forwarded to our support team.

📖 Admin Guide – GoldenVow

Everything you need to know to manage your fundraising events.

👑 You Are the Super Admin!

What you earn: 2% platform fee from EVERY contribution across ALL events.

Minimum withdrawal: Fees must reach at least KES 50 before you can withdraw.

What you control: Approve withdrawals, manage admins, lock/unlock events, system settings.

🧑‍💼 Admins

What they earn: The FULL pledge amount from contributors (the 2% fee goes to Super Admin).

What they do: Create events, manage contributors, approve/decline payments.

Event Locking: If your event's total fee reaches KES 50+ and you don't pay within 3 days, the event locks.

🔄 The Complete Money Flow

  1. Contributor sends money to the admin's account
  2. Admin approves the contribution on the system
  3. System deducts 2% – this goes to Super Admin
  4. Admin gets the full amount from the contributor
  5. Super Admin accumulates ALL 2% fees from ALL events
  6. Super Admin withdraws when fees reach KES 50+

📌 Important Rules

  • Event Locking: Events lock if total fee reaches KES 50 and is NOT paid within 3 days.
  • When Locked: NO ONE can access the event – not even the admin.
  • Unlocking: Admin pays Super Admin, then clicks "Request Unlock".
  • Super Admin Withdrawal: Only withdraw when fees reach KES 50 or more.
